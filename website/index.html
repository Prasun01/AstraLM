? FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

01What is AstraLM? +
AstraLM is an open-source on-device AI assistant for Android built with Flutter and native C++ inference engines (llama.cpp and Google LiteRT). It executes quantized models locally on your phone's processor with zero network dependence.
02Which models are supported locally? +
AstraLM supports curated GGUF models including DeepSeek-R1 Distill Qwen (1.5B), Qwen 2.5 Coder (1.5B/3B), Google Gemma 2 (2B), and SmolLM2 (1.7B), as well as custom GGUF files imported from your device storage.
03What are the hardware requirements? +
Any Android 8.0+ device with a 64-bit ARM processor (ARMv8 / ARM64). We recommend 6GB+ RAM for 1.5B-2B models and 8GB+ RAM for 3B models.
04Does AstraLM collect any data? +
Zero. AstraLM has zero telemetry, zero trackers, and zero analytics. All chat history, models, and tokens stay strictly in your local device storage.
05Is AstraLM free and open source? +
Yes! AstraLM is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. You can inspect the code, build from source, or download the pre-built signed APK from GitHub.
